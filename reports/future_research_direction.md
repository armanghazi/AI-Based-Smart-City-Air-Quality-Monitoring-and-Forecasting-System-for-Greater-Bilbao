# Future Research Directions

Several methodological improvements may enhance future versions of this project.

## Advanced Spatial Modeling

Due to the limited number of monitoring stations, traditional interpolation methods such as IDW or ordinary Kriging may produce oversmoothed pollution surfaces and edge-effect artifacts. Future work may explore machine learning-based spatial regression techniques.

## Land Use Regression (LUR)

Future implementations could integrate:

- Road network density
- Industrial land use
- Distance to coastline
- Population density
- Traffic intensity

to improve urban pollution estimation.

## Environmental Covariates

Additional environmental predictors may include:

- Digital Elevation Models (DEM)
- Wind speed and direction
- Temperature inversions
- NDVI vegetation indices
- Meteorological reanalysis datasets

These variables could improve the understanding of atmospheric dispersion and topographic confinement effects in the Bilbao estuary.

## Spatial Machine Learning

Advanced models such as:

- Random Forest Spatial Regression
- XGBoost
- Geographically Weighted Regression (GWR)

could be explored to generate more accurate pollution prediction surfaces and support Smart City environmental intelligence systems.
